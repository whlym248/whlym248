# Hi, I'm Yimin Liu

Undergraduate student exploring the intersection of pharmacy, biological science, protein engineering, and AI agents.

My current focus is building strong foundations in both life science and software engineering. I am especially interested in creating transparent, reproducible tools for scientific workflows rather than treating AI as a black box.

## Projects

### EnzyPilot

A local, CPU-friendly prototype for exploring enzyme and protein engineering workflows.

- Parses high-level engineering goals into structured tasks
- Analyzes PDB structures and relevant residue context
- Plans candidate mutations and generates readable reports
- Emphasizes traceability, reproducibility, and modular workflow design

> EnzyPilot is a learning-oriented engineering demo, not an experimentally validated enzyme design platform.

### Study Planner Agent

A learning productivity agent and web app prototype designed to turn academic commitments into practical daily plans.

- Course schedule import and deadline tracking
- Daily study plan generation
- Personalized study suggestions
- A simple workflow for keeping plans current as priorities change
